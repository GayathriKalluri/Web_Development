presence and frequency penality:

presence_penalty:
numbers from -2 to 2
defaults to 0
higher numbers increases a models likelihood of talking about new topics

frequency_penalty: this will reduce the repeating words
numbers from -2 to 2
defaults to 0
higher numbers it decreases a models likelihood of repeating the exact same phrase
